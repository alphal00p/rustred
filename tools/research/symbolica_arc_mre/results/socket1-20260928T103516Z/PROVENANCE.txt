Caveats found in review (not visible from the files alone):
- Binaries: sweep/ and profile/ = vendored checkout 953e26e2 WITH an uncommitted heap_pow change inside
  src/poly/polynomial.rs (+108/-22 lines, so line numbers in the binary's debug info are shifted by ~43
  lines relative to the committed file); upstream-445b882d/ = pristine dev 445b882d (sha a586cf96...);
  patched-proposed/ = dev 445b882d + the final patch/proposed.diff (sha d2c7de4b...);
  patched-proposed-v1/ = dev 445b882d + an earlier revision of proposed.diff (sha d473ea85...), whose diff
  was not kept. session-driver.sh wrote the v1 results into patched-proposed/; that directory was renamed to
  patched-proposed-v1/ by hand before session-driver-3.sh re-measured the final diff into patched-proposed/.
  Patched and pristine dev builds report the same version string (symbolica-v3.0.0-25-g445b882d).
- Cache-line layout: the programs of this session did not pin or report it. Checked afterwards with gdb
  (binary 2e8e100e, break at check_on_map): for the argument shapes of run.sh/profile.sh the shared
  context's ArcInner is at line offset 0 (strong, weak and the `variables` pointer in one line); with one
  extra argument (`--fixed 3`, the default value) it is at offset 48 (pointer on the next line). The
  variable map's ArcInner was at offset 0 in our gdb runs and at 32 in a reviewer's, i.e. it also depends
  on the environment; its offset during the measured runs is not known. A reviewer's A/B on socket 0
  (K=40) found the offset-48 context layout to roughly halve the shared penalty. The later session pins
  and reports both offsets.
- perf counters in runs.jsonl cover the whole process (set-up and the OPS/10 warm-up included), so the
  per-operation cycles/instructions/fills in summary.md are over-estimates by at least 10%.
- The process was not started on the first run CPU (globals and early main-arena pages could be homed on
  another NUMA node); numa_balancing was 1.
- The unpatched and patched numbers come from different trees (vendored vs dev) and different parts of the
  session (foreign busy load ~21% in sweep/, ~48% in patched-proposed/). Repeats: 2; the largest spread is
  11% (patched-proposed shared full K=96: 37,471 vs 41,615 ns).
