# Critical geometry: bounded terminal diagnostics, 2026-10-08

## Result and recommendation

The existing public `sector::masters::MasterCounter` was sufficient for the
first diagnostic. No new algebra engine or production code was implemented.
Two oracle-shortlisted four-loop sectors both returned `Counted(2)` before
symmetry identification. Neither supplied a non-isolated-locus signal that
would justify a magic-sector search in this experiment.

Prioritize exact cross-parent scalar column identification and ordinary IBP
replay. The offline oracle reported dot-Euler patterns with multiplicities
`4+2+1` and `4+4`; these nominate sums of native diagonal IBP rows at the
undotted corners. Oracle coefficients must not enter that construction.
Adding a diagonal-row sum at an already processed seed cannot enlarge its
ordinary row span: the potential gain is identifying previously separate
dotted columns across parent families, or supplying a genuinely absent seed.
One further oracle combination involving lower sectors still needs an exact
uncut row-space experiment. These recommendations are inferences, not results
of an implemented cross-family reducer.

## Literature and mathematical boundaries

- [Lee–Pomeransky, 1308.6676v2](https://arxiv.org/html/1308.6676v2), section 4:
  the saturated ideal is generated by the derivatives of `G` and `t*G-1`.
  For an isolated proper critical locus, its quotient dimension retains
  multiplicities and precedes symmetry identification. A positive-dimensional
  quotient is not an infinite-master conclusion; further geometry is needed.
- [Page–Song, 2509.17681v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.17681v1), sections 3–5:
  the critical-syzygy sufficiency argument concerns isolated critical loci on
  the maximal cut and the large-epsilon limit. Their practical construction
  checks saturation multiplicity and uses finite polynomial coefficient
  constraints. A cut relation is not an uncut relation with its lower-sector
  terms deleted; full divergence and all boundary contributions need replay.
- [Crisanti et al., 2605.29789v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.29789v2), sections
  4.3–4.4: the proposed magic generators satisfy both
  `sum(phi_i*d_i G)=0` and `sum(d_i phi_i)=0`. General equivalence with
  positive-dimensional critical varieties remains conjectural, and the
  discussion states additional gcd hypotheses. The latest public revision
  checked here is September 16, 2026. Its ancillary implementation is not a
  production dependency of this experiment.

### Native versus Euclidean mass convention

Native `family/symanzik/construction.rs` constructs
`F=U*c-q^T adj(A)q`, where `c=sum(constant(D_i)*x_i)`. Thus a vacuum has
`G=U*(1+c)` and Euler homogeneity implies `c=-L/(L+1)` at a proper critical
point. For the stored convention `D_i=q_i^2-1`, this is
`sum(x_i)=+L/(L+1)`, not minus. Calling the constant-weighted quantity `s`
recovers `G=U*(1+s)` and `s=-L/(L+1)`.

Correspondingly, with `D_i=q_i^2-m^2`, mass scaling gives
`m^2*sum(a_i*I(a+e_i))=(L*d/2-sum(a_i))*I(a)`; the Euclidean
`q_i^2+m^2` convention reverses that sign. Any implementation must derive the
row from actual native derivative contractions, including auxiliary slots,
instead of assuming the displayed convention for every family. A uniform
parameter sign change preserves critical geometry but is not permission to
ignore signs in integral identities.
