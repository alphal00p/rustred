# Physics-shaped demand and boundary reductions for the five-loop campaign

Date: 15 October 2026. Research and source audit only. No production mutation,
IBP generation, solver pilot, new master evaluation, or algebra implementation
was performed by this lane. Work and evidence paths are under
`/shared/codex-2/rustred`.

## Executive conclusion

The reported approximately101 million discovered domains, 3.5 million recorded
closed domains and 0/116 closed requests are a motivation to investigate, not
proof of an infinite reduction or missing IBPs. These are symbolic-region
obligations, not distinct physical integrals. The counts were supplied to this
research task; this lane did not independently poll the live campaign.

Three directions merit discriminating tests, in this order:

1. **Selective exact finite-demand discharge using the implementation already
   present**, rather than forcing every small finite request through a large
   symbolic-region graph. This is the smallest scope-preserving experiment.
2. **An original-graph/Taylor-jet demand profile**, retaining denominator
   multiplicities and derivative-placement correlations instead of replacing
   them by only A/R/D maxima. This has greater potential to remove irrelevant
   starting work, but requires an explicitly different physical-input profile
   and a coverage proof; it must not silently narrow the116 frozen boxes.
3. **Targeted sector-boundary block relations**, choosing the worst genuine
   surviving exact demands and eliminating expensive excursions before
   recursively expanding their lower-sector tails. This uses TIDE/Kira/Blade
   principles but retains RustRed's exact source and guard authority.

I do not recommend another blind ordering sweep, global dimensional-shift
composition, blanket root enumeration, or a larger core count as a substitute
for these tests. The literature supports the mechanisms below, not a predicted
five-loop speedup or guaranteed completion time.

## 1. What is actually being covered

The independently prepared campaign retains116 original-template requests over
67 owners. They have exact D=9/D=10 bands, global R<=6 and the justified
topology-dependent upper limits. There are no auxiliary starting helpers. It
does **not** ask for all arbitrary integer keys with D<=10, and it does not
amount to a census of every physical input after massive-basis lowering.
See [the launch and scope note](minimal_r6_d10_campaign_2026-10-14.md).

For original massless Feynman-gauge QCD kinetic/interaction counterterms,
the integrated highest homogeneous coefficient has physical grade2L. At five
loops this is10. Proper ordered forest contributions separate into lower-loop
tensor blocks, while the bare whole-graph highest Taylor coefficient is the
remaining genuinely coupled five-loop block. The aggregate connected envelope
A<=16-V4min and R<=6-V4min is safe for the clarified original-input scope,
but is deliberately an overapproximation of actual derivative placements and
numerator support. The paper's combined UV/soft prescription must be used;
ordinary second-order auxiliary-mass terms cannot be counted independently
without their soft overlap. [Local Unitarity, sections4.2.2 and5.1.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.11038).

Physical grade and native index sum differ after scalar lowering. For a term
(M^2)^j I_D, physical logarithmic grading reads D-j=10. Current Vakint internally
lowers scalar products and checks surviving keys individually, even if the
user never rewrites or cancels a numerator. Neither a new profile nor a tighter
queue must pretend that those terms disappear. It must retain exact lowering
provenance or explicitly admit and discharge all required images.

### What a rank-zero pilot does and does not test

In a sector with t positive slots, A>=t. Therefore R=0 and D<=10 leave sectors
with t>10 empty. The11-line parents first appear at R=1 in D10; a12-line parent
first appears at R=2 in D10 and R=3 in D9. A successful R0 campaign would be
valuable, but would not establish scalar coverage of all67 topologies.

After the root agent's R0 pilot, a useful independent small control is the
minimal-rank corner R=max(0,t-10) in each admissible D10 sector, with unit
positive powers and the remaining exact requested bounds. This touches the
12-line parents without launching their full R6 envelope. Its success remains
only this explicitly bounded scope.

## 3. Proposal A: selective exact finite-demand discharge

### Mechanism

Use the existing whole-initial-domain finite replay option for requests that
are genuinely small under exact coordinate/A/R/D intersection. Enumerate every
allowed root in such a selected request, follow exact saved-rule and routing
transitions, share concrete subproblems, and publish the existing replayable
summary. Other requests continue through ordinary symbolic traversal. Keep the
same frozen rules, terminal conventions and complete required-input union.

**Current capability limit:** the implemented v2 option is fresh-campaign-only
and restricted to the whole original first query/ID0. Its permitted geometry
is a singleton or an exactly equal untruncated finite A/R/D envelope, not an
arbitrary residual or G2 piece. It does not yet implement the automatic
multi-request selector described above. The immediate no-new-feature test
places one fixed complete eligible request first in a separate diagnostic;
broader adaptive integration would require explicit implementation and audit.
Do not add this option to an existing production resume command.

This is a choice of representation of an obligation, not a new IBP algorithm.
An exact replay can be preferable even if it visits more cheap concrete keys
than symbolic nodes. Conversely, large numerator transport can make it worse.
Do not deduce its cost from root cardinality alone.

A successful concrete traversal follows strict native descent at fixed support
and drops support before recanonicalizing. Under those checked premises, finite
branching and finitely many roots imply a finite reachable tree. This is a
conditional termination argument, not a practical cardinality bound or ETA.
No corresponding conclusion follows for arbitrary broadened symbolic boxes.

### Smallest test and guarantee

Use the same prepared R0 input and binaries in paired runs: finite replay off
and selective existing finite replay on. Compare complete requested closure,
whole native time including the same publication boundary, peak memory,
physical keys, route expansions, failed/unused attempts and checkpoint cost.
Then repeat on the fixed per-owner minimal-rank corner and one bounded R1/R2
region where both routes can actually close.

Require independent exact replay of every discharged seed. Every unresolved
seed remains an obligation, never a new master. No source cap applies to
descendants. A failed replay must leave a valid fallback and clear resource
accounting, rather than consume most of the campaign budget repeatedly.

**Falsifier:** complete same-scope time does not improve, or numerator transport
dominates as in H1. Park automatic promotion after one evidence-backed correction;
keep the option for genuinely favorable small requests. This cannot be promoted
from a graph-node-count decrease alone.

**Genericity:** the selection is by exact demand cardinality and observed
transition work, never loop count, topology name or preferred benchmark.

## 4. Proposal B: original-graph and Taylor-jet demand, not a rectangular physics box

### Missing information in the current profile

For an original graph, let m_e record the actual multiplicity of each equal-
momentum propagator before its powers are merged. Choose a routing of external
momenta compatible with the required Taylor operation. A quadratic Taylor
coefficient only produces denominator increments delta_e with

    a_e = m_e + delta_e, delta_e>=0, sum_e delta_e<=2,

and increments occur only on externally shifted lines. Numerator derivatives
consume Taylor degree instead. Two first derivatives or one second derivative
have specific numerator factors, so denominator increments and numerator
support are correlated. The aggregate A<=16/R<=6 bounds forget this provenance
and permit many independent dot placements and numerator assignments.

The equation above describes the original whole-graph quadratic Taylor jet,
not arbitrary IBP descendants or a fully flattened forest. It must be adjusted
for the lower Taylor order of other QCD kinetic/interaction vertices. Repeated
momenta must retain multiplicities; a set of occupied slots is insufficient.

### Concrete generic architecture

1. Enumerate the already specified renormalizable cubic/quartic vacuum
   skeletons, with allowed external-leg attachments and field/derivative-degree
   assignments. This is universal for the theory class, not the selected
   diagrams of one beta-function amplitude.
2. Construct a finite **support-only Taylor jet** using exact native polynomial
   and graph services. Retain where derivative degree went, original edge
   multiplicity, external routing and the homogeneous numerator module. A
   deliberately conservative numerator-support generator is allowed if its
   inclusion proof is explicit.
3. Factor every proper forest into lower-loop tensor blocks before assigning
   a genuinely five-loop request. Cross-block contractions are handled exactly;
   they are not replaced by scalar products of already reduced factors.
4. Lower each allowed physical polynomial to the chosen native family with
   explicit mass grading/provenance. Union the finite exact supports or a
   certified compact cover. A compact cover must preserve the correlations
   that motivated the change; turning it immediately back into the same
   A/R/D box would gain nothing.
5. Reuse saved parametric rules unchanged at first. Any local missing image is
   exposed as a concrete failure, not silently omitted or called a master.

This resembles the demand-specific subfamily organization advocated by modern
reduction tools, but the proposed Taylor-jet representation and its completeness
proof are RustRed design work, not a theorem supplied by those papers.

### Scope guarantee and falsifier

For every original graph allowed by the declared renormalizable model/routing
class, prove that each highest Taylor coefficient belongs to the emitted
numerator/denominator support union. This proves physical coverage without
having to solve all arbitrary polynomials in the old envelope. It does not
retain the literal old116-domain contract; the user must approve the replacement
profile and its precise claim. Do not modify the current campaign to implement
this silently.

The smallest discriminator is an offline four-loop support census, before any
new solve: compare the jet union against the existing physical box; verify
native preparation for a small independently generated set of actual gluon,
ghost and quark graphs, repeated lines, and proper self-energy forests. Measure
both root reduction and added provenance/union complexity. For five loops,
repeat first on two fixed skeleton classes with contrasting V4min.

**Falsifier:** the union over all allowed attachments nearly fills the original
box, or its exact representation fragments so severely that a full four-loop
walk is slower. Then retain the original envelope; a narrower QCD amplitude
catalogue remains outside the user's requested scope.

**Genericity:** graph valences, derivative degree and allowed vertex data are
inputs; the engine has no five-loop/QCD-name branches. External fermions and
ghosts are covered through supplied tensor/degree data, not handwritten IBPs.

## 5. Proposal C: boundary blocks that avoid expensive mapped-sector excursions

### What the sources suggest

TIDE distinguishes wanted integrals from a generated halo, postpones lower-
sector elimination, and uses no-rank-growth/no-dot-growth relations specifically
at seed boundaries. It keeps small coupled recurrences when scalar decoupling
inflates coefficients. These are complementary mechanisms, not evidence that
the thesis solved RustRed's symbolic-region completion problem.
[Luthe thesis, sections2.4,5.2,8.1.2--8.1.4](https://noah.nrw/ubbihs/download/pdf/5131192).

Kira3 selects forward-elimination dependencies and repairs remaining targets;
it also generates IBPs in symmetry-mapped sectors because numerator-bearing
symmetry expansions may be more expensive than direct IBPs. Its truncation
choices are checked and tuned rather than used as a theorem that all omitted
integrals vanish. Beware its notation: the paper's dot count d is neither
spacetime dimension nor RustRed's D=A-R.
[Kira3, sections3.1--3.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.20197).

Blade builds relations among a selected integral set and solves them in
blocks. Its spanning-sector procedure explicitly has an unproved assumption
about finding all magic relations. That part is unsuitable as silent RustRed
closure authority. Its block selection and moderate-rank tuning are useful
discovery strategies, with independent exact replay still required here.
[Blade, sections2.4--2.7 and3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2405.14621).

### Concrete candidate

Identify one current high-cost exact incoming demand to a sector, not merely
an abstract SCC or a frequently counted route. Retain that sector's native
coordinate frame temporarily, so its tensor/numerator polynomial is not expanded
into a different owner's frame before discovering whether local IBPs remove
it. Define a small block B of actual target keys/charts and a fixed seed halo.

Over several generic finite-field samples, use Symbolica sparse reduction to
eliminate columns that would create the measured expensive excursion. Keep
already available lower-sector relations as immutable coefficient-free
discovery references where justified; do not recursively apply every lower
tail during candidate search. Extract the direct source dependency trace only
after obtaining a candidate for B. Materialize/reconstruct it with Symbolica,
replay against original IBPs, and restore every guard and lower-sector tail.

At a rank boundary, first try complete ordinary-source combinations that cancel
rank-increasing terms. At a dot boundary, try the existing syzygy/source services
after another Symbolica public-API audit. These are choices of equations, not
permission to truncate the application domain. A fixed-basis block need not
minimize masters; it must reduce actual external demand to already supported
smaller targets or an explicitly finite boundary.

### Test and stopping rule

The four-loop candidate must contain genuine nonliteral routing; the ordinary
four-parent controls alone can have zero routed masks. Select one complete,
predeclared incoming cohort, keeping all source rules and requested points.
Then test one fixed five-loop cohort chosen from current cost evidence. Charge
candidate preparation, exact verification, all side exits, recursive work,
cold replay and storage. A shorter RHS or an improved modular rank is not the
success criterion.

**Falsifier:** no block pivot appears in the fixed halo; candidate replay fails;
new guards increase case splitting; or the full shared cohort gets no worthwhile
end-to-end gain. Permit one independently justified halo adjustment, then park.
The prior752-row24-term miss and broad-rule installation regressions remain
negative evidence. A no-frontier campaign is not improved by merely deriving
another valid rule that takes the same long path.

**Genericity:** choose blocks by measured incoming demand, immutable program
identity, routing support and source incidence. No hard-coded sector, loop
count, copied FORM rule or custom rational reconstruction kernel is required.

## 6. What not to conflate with these proposals

- **Mass grading:** exact homogeneous routes can retain D bounds, but affine
  constants can legitimately raise raw D. The existing upper-D routing proposal
  should be evaluated as written; it is not a new discovery here. Introducing
  a mass-grade tag can preserve correlation, but cannot justify clipping
  descendants to physical starting bounds.
- **Terminal minimality:** a finite larger terminal basis remains acceptable.
  Master-count diagnostics cannot prove the finite requested reduction closes.
- **Global dimensional shifts:** previously measured dense compositions are
  parked. A genuinely small subloop identity may be nominated by new cost
  evidence, but not by renaming the failed global approach.
- **Physics poles only:** high-D massive integrals with negative overall UV
  degree have poles determined by proper subdivergences, provided the
  subtraction scheme and auxiliary-mass counterterms are handled correctly.
  This can support a future MS-beta-only lane, but does not give their finite
  LU add-backs or a full reusable symbolic master decomposition. The current
  objective must not be silently replaced with that easier pole-only task.
  [Generic-numerator R operation, sections3--4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1703.03776),
  [auxiliary-mass counterterms, section2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-ph/9711266).

## 7. Recommended decision after the independent R0 control

If R0 does not close cheaply, first discriminate representation cost using
proposal A on a fixed small complete region. If finite and symbolic routes
both hit the same hard exact numerator transport, proposal C targets that
specific transition. If even R0 is affordable but R1/R2 rapidly broadens the
worklist, quantify the graph/Taylor support gap of proposal B before adding
more global rules.

Do not promise a restart until a matched complete pilot demonstrates the gain.
A failed small test should be preserved, not turned into an ever-expanding
research campaign. The first deliverable is a scoped, reusable exact reduction
for the chosen input profile; numerical masters and minimality remain later.
