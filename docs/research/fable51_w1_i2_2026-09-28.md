## 1. The rebuild

Tool: `crates/rustred-app/examples/route_witness_rewrite.rs` (e93c1214). A route maps source line j to owner momentum
`v_j T`, `T = source_to_representative · owner_to_representative⁻¹`; any owner loop automorphism M (unimodular,
permuting the owner's active lines up to sign; enumerated combinatorially, every inverse/product/determinant by
Symbolica's `Matrix` over Q, as in W0.6) gives another valid witness `T M`, written back as
`source_to_representative := (T M) · owner_to_representative`. Cancellation support = the owner active lines that
occur in the line-square expansion of some source irreducible numerator (one Symbolica inverse of the 15x15 basis).

- `--frame route` (default): the W0.6 per-route argmin. Reproduction [M]: `repro-route/selection.json` sha256
  `d9760837…` = `TMP/w0/inputs/i2/selection-i2.json`, byte-identical.
- `--frame owner` (this rebuild, pre-registered before any walk): for each owner o, over all automorphisms M of o, the
  key (Σ_r w_r·support_r(M), Σ_r w_r·terms_r(M), Σ_r support_r(M), Σ_r terms_r(M)) over the transported routes r into
  o (w_r = v2 gen-7 Route traffic of the source mask), lexicographic argmin, identity kept on ties. The owner's own
  identity route (`requires_transport` false; one per owner) is not touched. Every automorphism was valid on every
  route (0 invalid).

## 4. Mechanism [M: `c5f/inflow-ord1.json`, `mech/`]

Tool `tools/research/inputs/apply_inflow.py` splits the dependency edges INTO each owner's Apply domains by the frame
in which they arrive: A-self (Apply successors of the same owner, inheriting the parent's frame), A-other (another
owner's Apply successor on the installed owner mask, identity frame), R-self (Route on the owner's own mask, identity
route), R-transport (Route on another mask through its witness: the only frame the rewrite changes).

On C-5F every L* owner's Apply inflow is R-transport + A-self (only 011001000001111 also has A-other 13,411 and
R-self 362 edges), so with one frame per owner all routed images into an owner ARE mutually consistent. Yet (orig-ord1
→ i2b-ord1; Apply natives, R-transport edges, A-self edges):

| 111000010101001 | 121 of 144 | 43,081 → 141,258 (3.28x) | 11,717 → 12,456 | 604,101 → 1,797,134 |

- Routed inflow into 000011001001011 is unchanged edge for edge (R-transport 36,197 → 36,197), but its Apply natives
  go 59,585 → **144,611 (2.43x)** and its A-self edges 926,735 → 2,546,537 (2.75x).
- Every other owner is unchanged within ±0.01 %, except 000010011001001 (12,567 → 20,891 Apply natives, R-transport
  214,103 → 284,931): the extra Apply→Route sub-sector traffic of the rotated owner routes into it.
- Totals: natives 967,621 → **1,137,345 (1.175x)**, discovered 1,273,376 → 1,520,788 (1.194x), Apply natives 271,475
  → 364,838 (1.34x); Route→Route per Route native 4.137 → 4.124 (this owner carries little Route traffic in C-5F).

Reading [M for the counts, E for the explanation]: the extra work is created inside the rotated owner, from the same
routed covers, by the owner's own Apply rule applications. An owner's saved reduction program is not invariant under
its loop automorphisms: a routed cover whose positive-power and numerator pattern lands on permuted owner axes is
reduced through a larger cascade of Apply self-successors, which no longer nests into the containers the identity
frame produces. The frame inconsistency among routes (the W0.6 hypothesis) is not needed to produce the growth, and
removing it (i2b) does not remove the growth. Rotations are not uniformly harmful (010111011000001 1.01x under its
index-1 frame), so the cost is owner- and frame-specific, and the support metric does not predict it.

## 5. Consequences

- **Not shipped.** Inputs v4 / LC1 keep the original witnesses (`d2667dc9…`). `selection-i2b.json` and
  `selection-i2.json` stay research artifacts in TMP; nothing under `examples/input/` changes.
- **What a future I2-like lever would need** [E]: a frame choice scored by the owner's Apply cost, not by Route
  support: e.g. re-inspect a sample of each owner's routed covers under each candidate automorphism with the W0.3 K=1
  harness and accept a frame only where Apply natives and operations do not rise, gated again on drained controls.
  Its ceiling is small: under the one-frame constraint the Route-side gain on C-5F is −9 % Route→Route per Route
  native (the unconstrained per-route choice reached −61 %, but that choice also fragments Apply work), Route natives
  are ~1 % of K=1 native work (W0.6 §4.4), and absolute Route→Route edges rose in both rewrites. Not recommended for
  the launch timeline.
- The W0.6 follow-up alternative "change a route only when it strictly lowers support and keeps the owner frame" is
  covered by the mechanism result: any non-identity automorphism rotates the owner-side cover, which is where the cost
  arises [E]; it was not run.
