# Independent audit: profile-guided parametric rule optimization

Date: 2026-10-03. Scope: experimental design, public/core API boundaries, and
targeted primary-source research. This note does not certify a candidate or
claim an optimization result. Its author did not implement the new profiler or
comparison helper. No production mutation, checkpoint restore, whole-sidecar
scan, compilation, or native pilot was performed for this audit.

## Assessment

The plan is suitable for bootstrapping a research workflow, provided that
completed shared-cohort cost remains the promotion criterion. A completed A/A
control should precede candidate comparisons. A bounded prefix, local panel,
smaller recurrence, or first-20-minute campaign counter difference cannot by
itself establish an improvement. Incomplete work is censored, not inexpensive.

Read in full: [the optimization plan](../../PROFILE_GUIDED_RULE_OPTIMIZATION_PLAN.md),
[the new-rules study](../../CODEX_NEW_RULES_STUDY.md), and
[the previous selection portfolio](rule_selection_portfolio_2026-10-01.md).
The proposed gates below operationalize that plan; numeric regression thresholds
marked **proposed** need coordinator acceptance before observing candidate results.

There is no basis yet to claim the plan's at-least-20% repeatable completed-cohort
or end-to-end improvement. A successful workflow test establishes measurement
capability, not that any new rule is useful.

## Why the previous negative results matter

The prior portfolio supplies unusually strong warnings against local proxies:

- Under the same legacy descent order, rank-first quality selection cut immediate
  successors by about 21.4%, but increased complete A1 domains from 26,025 to
  29,768 and native inspections from 17,957 by about 18.05%. Additional routing
  work was a major component. A single-owner substitution transferred most of
  the Route increase, but that establishes a program-level effect, not a
  per-rule cost attribution.
- Six finite late panels and the early five-panel set preferred the rule-quality
  candidate even though the full campaign did not. A rule with unchanged native
  successor count could have different graph outdegree because of sharing.
  Summing isolated root cones would double-count shared descendants and miss
  interference through routing and containment.
- Positive-excursion and guard-piece features did not produce a useful complete
  gain. A pre-cut experiment reduced work only while a reference-off check
  exposed 557 uncovered obligations. Correctness cannot be inferred from a
  smaller graph, even when many local tuples agree.
- The prior 752-row targeted bank could not eliminate its nominated 24 hard
  terms jointly. Larger seed banks, affine averaging, and dimension-shift
  composites often increased fill or downstream work. Repeating the identical
  failed projected bank without a new rank-changing mechanism is not research.
- A reflection identity helped four-loop local endpoints but did not improve
  its five-loop complete runtime. A point with 46 RHS terms versus 18 under
  another order nevertheless introduced six terminal keys and worsened the
  complete required-root cohort. Pointwise brevity is not parametric coverage.
- The exact-key closure reference had zero hits in the tested cones. Equality
  against stale domain keys is therefore not a demonstrated novelty/cost
  estimator. A semantic containment hit can be useful only with separately
  established closure authority; a miss means unknown.

These facts are repository experiment reports, not external-paper claims. They
motivate candidate falsifiers and inclusion of routing/guard costs, not permanent
rejection of all related mathematical ideas.

## Preregistered validation gates

### 1. Freeze the comparison before seeing candidate outcomes

Record a content-addressed experiment specification containing:

- Required roots, query geometry including A/R/D constraints, all helper owners,
  routes, ordered terminal-key inventory, base owner bytes and ordered overlays.
  Final five-loop scope remains 116 required roots and 67 helper owners.
- Exact executable and steering hashes, engine/record semantics, one common
  strict-descent order, optional-rule placement policy, all resolver/coverage
  flags, worker count, CPU allocation, budgets, and cancellation policy.
- Cold versus warm initial state. If there is an inherited closure cache, bind
  its identity and proof scope identically in both arms. A candidate cannot
  inherit extra solved work for free.
- Discovery cohort, validation cohort, untouched held-out owner/case families,
  primary metric, cost boundary, stopping rule and regression thresholds.
- Candidate-generation recipe and budget, source proof/cold-validation receipt,
  precise candidate payload, and reason the mechanism differs from parked work.

Split holdouts by semantic owner/case family or independently nominated root
regions, not adjacent checkpoint byte windows. Related boxes from one recurrence
are strongly correlated. Publish all attempted candidates, including refusals,
timeouts and algebraically valid losers. Do not tune on the held-out result and
continue calling it held out.

### 2. Separate three distinct authorities

1. Exact source identity plus denominator/guard preservation and strict descent
   proves a rule valid on its declared domain.
2. Actual matching/application proves where that rule is selected in a frozen
   owner program; append-only repair overlays may never be considered where the
   baseline already covers the piece.
3. Complete recursive evaluation proves the nominated cohort reaches the same
   authorized terminal set without frontier or unresolved debt.

None implies the next. Finite numerical checks are useful falsifiers, not a
replacement for the first gate. Cold replay of a partial rule is not a whole-owner
coverage claim. Native inspector termination is not campaign completion.

Reject any candidate that adds residual master/terminal keys, drops helpers or
routes, weakens predicates, changes global descent order within a mixed program,
or relies on an unproved lower-sector/cut deletion. A whole-program alternative
ordering is a separately labeled experiment, not a local rule improvement.

### 3. Use a shared-union cost, with explicit completion state

Let C(P, Q, S) be the measured cost of running frozen program P on the entire
cohort Q from initial state S. Compare C(candidate, Q, S) with C(baseline, Q, S).
Do not approximate C(P, Q, S) by a sum of independently run single-root costs.

Maintain separate fields for emitted native successors, accepted native events,
distinct recorded edges, admitted nodes, Apply inspections, Route inspections,
guard/conditional pieces, route masks, duplicate emissions, containment/reuse,
coefficient work, wall time, process CPU and peak RSS. They are not synonyms.
In particular, `known_reuse` is not a closure count; a shared unresolved child
remains an obligation in the union graph.

Every arm needs an explicit outcome: complete, allowance-censored,
time-censored, cancelled, frontier, error, or invalid comparison. Missing fields
must not default to zero. Include pending nodes, in-flight work, failures,
frontiers, unresolved obligations and actual root publication/completion checks.
Completion requires all nominated roots handled and no unexplained outstanding
obligations, plus the existing authoritative completion/validation receipts.

Both arms must complete cleanly before a performance winner is possible.
Censored counts are lower bounds on total work, not final costs. Fixed-work and
fixed-time experiments can screen obvious losers or expose bottlenecks but
cannot promote a candidate merely because it did less before stopping. Keep the
incumbent on a tie, inconclusive result or failed validation.

For two proposed rule changes A and B, measure the combined program as well as
the individual changes. The interaction C(A+B)-C(A)-C(B)+C(base) may be nonzero.
Do not assemble a portfolio by summing individually estimated savings. A local
closed-cache hit is only reusable under matching program identity, phase and
semantics; self/ancestor references are not independent closure evidence.

### 4. Preserve the parent boundary and control fragmentation

Measure through the actual parent dispatch/matching step, not just its RHS list.
Record fallback selection and applicability on the baseline partition. Include
failed guard probes and all exceptional children. Whole-piece alternatives are
particularly valuable controls because they can retain the baseline partition
instead of introducing new pre-cuts.

Do not silently replace correlated A/R/D domains by coordinate hulls. An added
redundant-looking bound can alter routing or disable reuse, as the prior portfolio
observed. If both arms are intentionally repartitioned, name that treatment
separately and compare its total cost; do not attribute the difference solely to
the algebraic relation.

### 5. Stage operational and efficacy tests separately

1. Pure helper tests: reject incomplete/missing/deceptive receipts, mismatched
   terminal inventories, different frozen options and invalid metric scopes.
2. Complete four-loop A/A through the actual staging, supervision and cold gates.
   Exact outcome/authority equality is required. Record structural-count and
   timing variability; do not hard-code a false claim that parallel scheduling
   must produce bit-identical graph counts.
3. Candidate versus baseline on discovery/validation cohorts, then complete
   four-loop controls FG/BMW/H/X together and the preregistered five-loop cohort.
4. Finalists on untouched held-out five-loop regions, then the relevant full
   scope when feasible. A bounded incomplete whole-campaign prefix remains
   diagnostic even after a completed small-cohort win.

Use at least two matched counterbalanced pairs (A/B, then B/A), identical workers
and CPU class, and no overlap with live production CPUs64-127. Include supervisor
preparation, load, solve, cold replay and cleanup in the stated end-to-end
boundary. Report recurring solve cost separately from one-time generation and
amortization; inspector-seconds are neither CPU time nor critical-path wall time.

The plan's >=20% gate must be assessed on its preregistered primary metric, not
whichever count happens to improve. **Proposed conservative repeatability gate:**
both matched pairs favor the candidate and the pooled paired cost ratio is at
most 0.80; publish each pair rather than inventing a confidence interval from two
pairs. **Proposed material-regression screen:** no validated control/held-out
cohort worsens by more than 5% in its primary metric and no peak-RSS increase
over 10%, unless a different justified threshold was frozen beforehand. Wall
time noise may require more pairs; it cannot be solved by post-hoc metric choice.

### 6. Minimal adversarial comparer fixtures

- Candidate has fewer domains because it hit its allowance before any root
  completed: no winner.
- Both status files say native finished, but one result retains pending nodes or
  a frontier: no winner.
- Two roots share an expensive open descendant: charge the union once, not zero
  and not twice; do not infer its future cost from a lookup hit.
- Candidate silently adds a terminal key or drops a helper/route: invalid.
- Candidate wins RHS count but creates more Route or guard work: report the full
  cost vector and decide only with the preregistered completed metric.
- Different A/R/D boundary, initial cache, descent order, binary or measurement
  scope: invalid comparison, even if top-level labels match.
- Candidate and baseline are byte-identical A/A: outcome must be neutral, never
  an optimization result. Small scheduling noise is not a new rule benefit.

## CP6 profiling: permissible evidence and limitations

The proposed sampler reads 32 MiB total from sealed records of stopped campaigns,
with four 1 MiB windows at 1/8, 3/8, 5/8 and 7/8 of each preregistered segment.
Suggested generation strata are Oct1 [1,13,26,38] and Oct2 [1,9,17,25]. This is
purposeful byte-window discovery, not an unbiased domain or root census.
Large records, publication order, correlated neighbors and truncated boundaries
affect inclusion. The final segment is not necessarily representative of the
final semantic frontier population.

The draft `tools/research/rule_optimizer/profile_records.py` field layout was
cross-checked against `epoch/records/{wire,typed}.rs` and `ResolverCounters`.
Its explicit non-authoritative/sample-only labels are appropriate. Before a
sample is approved, the executor itself must recheck the plan schema and total
byte cap, not rely only on the planner; G2 count limits should match native
validation, and duplicate/overlapping windows need rejection or explicit
accounting. Final independent review verified these fixes, schema-1 metadata,
decoded-arity checks and the native 2^18 G2 anchor/piece limits; all six profiler
unit tests passed independently. This accepts the bounded diagnostic extraction,
not population weighting or closure authority. A three-consecutive-frame
synchronization check is sensible diagnostic
protection, not authentication of the whole segment.

Keep each retained representative's complete geometry, phase, scope, offsets,
frame digest, source segment identity and record diagnostics. Preserve window
hashes/offsets and skipped bytes so the aggregate is reproducible. Distinguish
cumulative campaign snapshots from record-local counters and interval deltas.
Selected rule identity, exact newly admitted children, required/helper ancestry
and recursive closure are not recoverable merely from these sampled records;
label them unavailable rather than inventing attribution.

## Relevant existing API boundaries

- `solver/sector/selection.rs`: `rule_trial` already performs exact rule finish
  and exceptional geometry admission. `select_rule` retains only the chosen
  candidate; `RuleTrialFinished` provides summary features, not a retained
  algebraic candidate. A future opt-in read-only candidate observer is a small
  possible boundary, but must not change FirstValid/default selection, queue
  mutation or loser/refusal accounting. Bound retention count/bytes explicitly.
- `solver/candidate_reduction/owners/domains/matching/engine/after_baseline.rs`:
  whole-piece alternatives run only after baseline selection, require uniform
  fixed coordinates/guards/denominators, retain correlation predicates, and do
  not call cut/refinement recursively. Unknown applicability falls back; typed
  resource/corruption/cancellation errors propagate rather than being swallowed.
- `owners/feedback/restore.rs`:
  `BoundOwnerSearch::restore_replayed_residual_free_overlay` regenerates sources,
  replays equations/declared guards and checks the owner's order. It rejects
  finite residual terminals. It deliberately grants neither requested-case nor
  recursive coverage. Search-policy metadata is provenance, not historical proof.
- `foundry/artifact/source_port/original_producer.rs`:
  `check_original_source_combination` accepts explicit full ordinary-source
  weights and reconstructs poles/conditions. It requires supported uncut
  support-primary order and coordinate boxes; it does not expose arbitrary affine
  exclusions. A rectangle must not stand in for an affine chart. Existing domain
  replay is the other relevant proof path for retained ordinary solver rules.
- `solver/candidate_reduction/power_domain/summary.rs`:
  `DomainPowerSummary::contains` exactly decides inclusion for its admitted
  fixed-support box-plus-A/R/D vocabulary. It explicitly proves no processing or
  coverage and requires independent phase/program binding. It is not a generic
  correlated-polyhedron containment oracle.
- `solver/discovery/target_only.rs` and
  `solver/discovery/semi_numerical/source_weights/probe.rs`: native target-block
  GPLU/L-weight solving exists, but their forbidden block is the existing ordered
  prefix. An arbitrary measured expensive lower-column block is not currently an
  exposed parameter. Reindexing a separate discovery matrix is different from
  changing the mathematical `IntegralOrder`.
- Vendored Symbolica/numerica supplies `SparseRowReducer`, sparse matrices,
  `Matrix::partial_row_reduce`, `row_reduce`, `solve_any`, and fraction-free
  operations. RustRed already wraps these services for rank/source weights.
  Prefer those native operations and existing exact reconstruction/replay; do
  not create another CAS or reconstruction library. The workspace pins Symbolica
  `~3.0.0` with a local vendor patch, so pinned source—not an older web API
  spelling—is authoritative for implementation.

## Three concrete mechanisms worth falsifying

These are proposed adaptations, not claims that the papers already optimize
RustRed's shared domain graph. None bypasses exact source proof, strict descent,
guards or complete-cohort evaluation.

### A. Profile-directed forbidden-block source circuits

Literature connection: Blade chooses a compact target set and searches relations
that reduce a harder block to an easier one, increasing a bounded polynomial
ansatz until sufficient relations are found. This supports targeting the desired
relation support rather than indiscriminately expanding an auxiliary system.
[Blade, section 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.14621v2#S3)

Adaptation: choose a measured expensive child block B from discovery-only data.
In a separate source matrix, let F contain ALL original non-descending columns
other than the target and let H contain the measured expensive lower columns B.
Keep the target column separate. Seek source weights w with
`w^T [S_F,S_H] = 0` and `w^T s_target = 1`. The full reconstructed tail must still
strictly descend under the unchanged global order. The projection is a discovery
constraint, never a new ordering/coverage authority.

Cheapest falsifier: for a frozen field/chart/source bank, test whether
`rank([S_F,S_H,s_target]) > rank([S_F,S_H])`. Over the specified exact coefficient
field this is the linear-algebra feasibility criterion for annihilating the
forbidden block while retaining the target. At a finite numerical sample it is
only a candidate screen: specialization can create exceptional dependencies, so
neither a sample hit nor a miss is a generic identity/impossibility proof. Poles
must also be excluded at every probe. Use independently chosen admissible points
as screening, then exact full source reconstruction. Only add sources that can
change the nominated block's span; otherwise park the unchanged 752-row-style
failure immediately.

Symbolica fit: native sparse GPLU and accepted-L weight recovery, followed by
existing original-source or domain replay. An app-level bounded matrix adapter
may suffice; the current target-prefix API cannot be relabeled an arbitrary-block
API. No production core extension is justified before a concrete bank succeeds.

Failure conditions: target normalization creates new index-dependent poles;
source-weight degrees/term counts or GPLU fill exceed budget; cancellation leaves
a much more expensive low-order tail; applicability fragments or never wins
selection; exact replay/descent fails; or complete cohort cost does not improve.
Predeclare a bounded block-relaxation schedule and incumbent fallback; do not
silently relax until a favorable benchmark appears.

### B. Cancellation-aware source selection and constrained symbolic operators

Kira 3 selects from forward-elimination dependency information to avoid importing
irrelevant backward-substitution dependencies that cancel. It then checks the
selected system's sufficiency numerically and expands when needed. Its authors
also report that random row reordering can densify elimination, so fewer selected
equations is not an unconditional performance win.
[Kira 3, section 3.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.20197v1#S3.SS2)

Smith and Zeng generate polynomial combinations of ordinary IBP operators with
propagator-power constraints, row-reduce them symbolically, and use small
partially symbolic target-neighborhood systems for missing rules. Their approach
is particularly relevant to keeping spectator indices parametric while targeting
a hard region. [Syzygy-constrained symbolic rules, sections 2.3 and 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.11140v1#S2.SS3)

Adaptation: first look for a cancellation-supported source subset/circuit within
the current exact bank. If projected block rank is insufficient, nominate a
small polynomial/operator seed extension that specifically supplies the missing
direction, with free spectators retained. This is a candidate generator, not a
reason to delete obligations that merely disappeared from a numerical trace.

Cheapest falsifier: compare projected ranks and exact source supports before a
walk; stop if the new seed direction is redundant, the target cannot isolate, or
the result is identical to the incumbent after exact normalization. Before exact
large elimination, bound ansatz degree, rows, nonzeros and source-weight slots.

Symbolica fit: polynomial coefficient collection, native matrix services and
existing exact source expansion/replay. A bounded coefficient ansatz is not a
complete polynomial-module solver. The module-intersection literature intersects
constraints to avoid raised propagator powers/dimension shifts; it does not imply
that a rational target pivot has no coefficient poles.
[Module intersection overview](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.06895)
Do not introduce a new general Groebner/module-intersection framework for this
pilot, and do not confuse “no doubled propagators” with “no guard denominators.”

### C. Exact small-subgraph reflection on a proved parametric chart

Affine loop-momentum transformations can relate sectors, with active-propagator
bijection, unit absolute Jacobian and preserved external scalar products. The
recent symmetry analysis connects such transformations with parameter-polynomial
permutations. This supports looking for exact local reflection identities, not
assuming that arbitrary numerator powers transform by permutation.
[Discrete symmetries, section 2.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.08332v1#S2.SS2)

Adaptation: revisit only a profiled region where a small two-line/subgraph
reflection cancels an expensive child pair on a nontrivial parameter chart.
The previous singleton reflection swapped active axes and preserved one numerator,
but an adjacent exponent did not obey the same relation. Free spectator powers,
the complete transformed numerator polynomial and all chart restrictions must be
proved explicitly. Average an orbit only after predicting its exact support;
the prior broad averaging produced many more hard terms.

Cheapest falsifier: symbolic propagator/numerator substitution followed by one
adjacent-exponent and one off-chart countercheck; reject a proposed broad chart
at the first mismatch. Then compare the exact collected hard-block support before
any downstream walk. A concrete symmetry certificate cannot be passed off as an
ordinary IBP source-combination certificate; use an existing valid proof path or
stop for the missing proof boundary.

Symbolica fit: exact polynomial substitution/collection and existing RustRed
symmetry transport checks. No numerical integration or a new geometric package
is needed for the falsifier. The previously observed five-loop runtime non-win
means this mechanism deserves a new pilot only if profiling identifies a different
high-cost parametric region, not merely the same short identity again.

## Research claims that should not be imported uncritically

Target-conditioned seed priorities learned on simpler examples can propose source
ordering, but success at finding a smaller sufficient seed set is not success at
minimizing the shared descendant graph. The explainable-AI paper itself uses
target-distance-style seed objectives; such a heuristic should be a bounded
proposal with semantic holdouts, not a replacement for the completed evaluator.
[Explainable AI-assisted seed optimization](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.09544v1)

SAILIR is useful as a warning about both search objectives and measurement
boundaries: its greedy example can reduce a local count while increasing integral
weight, and a comparison table distinguishes ideal parallel chain time from
total Kira time and per-worker from total memory. Do not transport those speedup
figures into this task's matched wall/CPU/RSS protocol.
[SAILIR](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.05034v1)

The official Symbolica matrix documentation confirms native exact matrix/rational
polynomial support. Its 3.0 release changed some high-level solving APIs; inspect
the pinned local implementation before adopting an example signature.
[Matrices](https://symbolica.io/docs/matrices.html),
[Symbolica 3.0](https://symbolica.io/posts/symbolica_3_0_release/index.html)

## Structural alternative suggested by the two hot owners

The source denominator list in `examples/input/tide_five_loop.toml` supplies a
more specific lead than edge count. The saved selection fingerprint names that
common basis, but a native loader must still bind the family before generating
any identity. The following is an exact symbolic inference from the listed
momenta, not a native-generated rule or a claim of scalar factorization with
arbitrary numerators.

For `101010000110001`, the active denominators are D1,D3,D5,D10,D11,D15:
`k1²-1`, `k3²-1`, `k5²-1`, `(k2-k4)²-1`, `(k2-k5)²-1`, `(k3-k4)²-1`.

`p_k=0`, `p_r=aP*bJ_r-aJ*bP_r`. This p is independent of the differentiated k

```text
V_s = sum_r (p_s*g_r - g_s*p_r) * derivative_contraction(P,k,q_r)
```
