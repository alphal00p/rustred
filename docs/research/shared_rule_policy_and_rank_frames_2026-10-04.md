## 1. Choose whole rules jointly, with sharing-aware costs

### Literature and its limits

Roy, Seshadri, Sudarshan and Bhobe represent alternative query plans by an
AND/OR DAG and optimize reuse across several queries. Their greedy strategy
recomputes the benefit of shared intermediates; optimizing each query and only
then finding common subexpressions misses choices available to joint planning.
This is a database result, not an IBP algorithm.
[Efficient and Extensible Algorithms for Multi Query Optimization, §§2–4](https://www.cse.iitb.ac.in/~sudarsha/Pubs-dir/mqo-sigmod00.pdf).

Sun, Zhang and Ni connect equality-graph extraction to weighted monotone
circuits: an alternative is an OR choice, but all children of the chosen
operation are required. General optimal extraction is hard; their exact
algorithm is parameterized by treewidth and requires an acyclic extracted
derivation. RustRed's abstract containment cycles cannot simply be removed or
assigned zero cost to meet that condition.
[E-Graphs as Circuits, §§1–3](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.17042).

### Concrete RustRed mechanism

Use an input-declared subset of an already admitted preferred program, keeping
the unchanged baseline program as fallback. Each policy chooses **whole saved
rules**, never individual RHS edges. Preserve baseline terminals, explicit
preferred-residual holes, guard applicability, strict-error propagation and all
native dependencies. Score the union reached from the original four roots with
a fresh native walk, not the sum of per-rule fanouts. Both payloads and the
ordinal subset must be immutable checkpoint inputs.

The selected prospective experiment is the subset `{110}`; its decision record
exists, but final executable-bound requests are not frozen yet. It changes
the two starting choices together while avoiding the unselective all-preferred
intervention that failed. A narrow subset is not a topology-specific engine
special case: ordinals are input data bound to the payload digest.

## 2. A finite-rank numerator-frame atlas, not global delayed routing

### Literature and prior negative

Kira3 reports that numerator-bearing symmetry relations can become more
expensive than ordinary IBPs, and therefore also seeds mapped sectors. This
shows that identifying equivalent topologies does not make transport cheap.
Its reduced subsector seed bounds are generation heuristics, not permission
to discard needed descendants.
[Kira3, §§3.1.1–3.1.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.20197v1).

Frellesvig exhibits different loop orders requiring different numbers of extra
Baikov coordinates; §3.1, the paragraph introducing Eq.(17), also chooses extra
propagators for which exchanging the loop momenta preserves the whole set.
That is evidence that representation choices matter;
it is not evidence that RustRed should use Baikov integration at runtime.
[Loop-by-Loop Baikov Representation, §§3.1–3.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.01804v2).

The repository already proposed
[sector-local charts](sector_local_coordinate_chart_2026.md). Moreover, global
local-reduction-before-routing **failed** the combined4L test:26,025→27,256
domains and17,957→19,087 inspections. Neither a renamed helper nor another
global delay switch is a new proposal.

### Distinct hypothesis and exact boundary

The larger, distinct intervention would be to retain a **finite-rank polynomial
numerator in a sector-local frame through several transformations**, instead
of repeatedly expanding it into parent-family monomials. The frame is selected
jointly for frequent incoming/outgoing maps, not just for one sparse source row.
For example, transporting `(y+z)^R` into `x=y+z` takes one numerator coordinate
instead of first creating `R+1` monomials. This is an illustrative scalar
polynomial example, not a measured five-loop map.

Use a filtered representation, not a maximal-cut quotient that discards
pinches. If an active denominator is `D` with power `a`, terms containing
`D^a` belong to a lower-sector component; they must be retained alongside the
remainder. With finite numerator rank and bounded denominator powers this is
a finite stratified polynomial transport problem. General affine numerator
substitution is **not** a linear map on integral index vectors. Arbitrary free
numerator exponents still cannot be transported as a fixed finite-shift rule.

Every frame requires an invertible full-coordinate/completion witness, retained
determinant and kinematic-denominator conditions, exceptional faces, and exact
transport of caps and the common descent order. Finite starting bounds make
one polynomial transfer finite; they do not establish closure under subsequent
IBPs or repeated frame changes.

**Smallest falsifier, before an engine change.** Find one genuinely expensive
native Route transfer with its exact witness and requested finite-rank basis.
Compare complete expanded images, including lower-sector components, under the
existing frame and two structurally chosen ISP completions. Count distinct
required downstream domains and transport coefficients for the *whole basis*,
not a favorable polynomial. If no meaningful exclusive work disappears after
all conversion and boundary costs, leave this parked. The current observer
measures Apply successors, not the internals of their subsequent Route
transfers, so it does not supply this evidence yet.

**Cost.** High for production: a generic typed polynomial/frame payload,
filtered transport, caps, ordering and persistence support. A diagnostic is
medium cost if it can reuse existing exact routing maps and Symbolica
substitution. No custom polynomial engine, no new tensor reducer, no positive
auxiliary-denominator poles, and no new terminal authority. This remains
research, not a reason to postpone the inexpensive policy experiment.

## 3. Bounded boundary blocks: reuse a transfer, not a guessed universal basis

Blade constructs smaller block-triangular reduction systems for selected
integrals; it distinguishes rank partitions and supports reducing linear
combinations. Song retains reductions as products of low-complexity
intermediate-basis matrices rather than expanding their product. These results
support carefully chosen interfaces between elimination stages, not a theorem
that RustRed's symbolic worklist becomes finite.
[Blade, §§3.1–3.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.14621v2),
[Taming Symbolic IBP Reduction, §II](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.22500v1).

**Hypothesis.** For an actually repeated, finite-rank block, eliminate only
internal integral states and preserve the complete external boundary as a
reusable sparse transfer circuit. Known source circuits and authenticated
zero-sector evidence remain attached; keep coefficient products factored until
exact collection is needed. This is not a new master basis, not an unbounded
Macaulay/Janet completion, and not a finite sample extrapolated to free indices.

Merely evaluating several inputs together saves nothing if ordinary memoization
already shares every child. A useful block must eliminate expensive obligations
through exact cancellation or a better complete transfer. Nor may cancellations
between unrelated requested input columns narrow the universal numerator scope:
the transfer must be correct for every column independently.

An elimination block additionally needs a proved invertible pivot minor on its
declared domain, with the resulting poles and exceptional conditions retained.
An abstract graph cycle alone is not evidence that such elimination is valid.

**Smallest falsifier.** Start with existing complete raw outcomes, identify a
candidate block and count every side exit and shared external dependency. Reject
it structurally unless appreciable exclusive work could disappear. Only then
permit a bounded Symbolica modular rank/zero screen, followed by exact circuit
replay and a complete native walk. Modular zero is nomination only; original
source/weight poles and finite exceptional cases must survive. The previously
tested small diamond, hidden-zero and corridor motifs did not offer this
opportunity, so this proposal does not reopen those failures without a new
larger block witness.

**Cost.** Medium to high: selected-row replay is available, but a full-image
composition bridge retaining zero-sector tails, guards and block boundary
semantics is not yet the same thing as the read-only circuit observer. It must
not be implemented merely because small-matrix elimination sounds inexpensive.

## Recommendation and what remains unproved

Execute the existing-rule110 policy test first, followed by the preregistered
450 and joint secondary tests, not automatic deployment. The frame atlas
has larger structural upside but needs a new high-cost routing witness before
implementation. Bounded transfer blocks need a larger exclusive-work witness
than the already rejected motifs.

The literature review did not reveal a theorem providing cheap automatic
closure of this frozen five-loop scope, or an ETA. Tube seeding, finite-frame
normal forms, dimension shifts, antichains and abstract acceleration were
already studied in the linked notes; their conditions and negative pilots
remain in force. Better rule policy may reduce work substantially, but no
current table establishes that it will. Production and its checkpoint were
unchanged throughout this research.
