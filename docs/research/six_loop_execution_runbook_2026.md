# Six-loop execution runbook

## Status and claim boundary

This is the proposed execution architecture for eventual six-loop,
single-scale vacuum parametric-IBP closure in RustRed. It is deliberately more
specific than a literature review, but it is not a claim that practical
six-loop closure has been achieved. No reviewed paper or public program proves
that its method closes the complete physical `L = 6`, `K = 21` manifest with
acceptable time and memory.

Published results establish important components: master-integral finiteness
for a fixed graph, parametric-annihilator and Euler-characteristic rank
descriptions, complete Baikov logarithmic-vector-field generators, rational
double-shift formulations, Macaulay/Pfaffian constructions, and increasingly
powerful symbolic-rule discovery. They do not supply RustRed's required
all-sector, all-rank, guard-complete, replayable `K = 21` artifact by
themselves. Relevant primary sources include:

- [Smirnov and Petukhov, finite master count](https://arxiv.org/abs/1004.4199);
- [Bitoun et al., parametric annihilators and Euler characteristic](https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.09215);
- [Boehm et al., complete logarithmic vector fields](https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.09737);
- [Barakat et al., rational double-shift Groebner formulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.05347);
- [Chestnov et al., Macaulay matrices](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.12983);
- [Smith and Zeng, syzygy-constrained symbolic rules](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.11140);
- [Feng et al., generating-function symbolic reduction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09541);
- [Page and Song, critical-point syzygies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.17681); and
- [Coro et al., compact determinantal syzygies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06365).

The architecture below is a RustRed proposal assembled from those results,
the LiteRed2 baseline, bounded measurements, and independent adversarial
audits. Stage 1 authorizes bounded high-loop studies only. It does not
authorize production of four- through six-loop artifacts until the active
three-loop/Vakint goal is complete and the user opens Stage 2.

The direct baseline references are Lee's original
[LiteRed presentation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1212.2685) and
[LiteRed 1.4 update](https://arxiv.org/abs/1310.1145).

## What `K` means

For an `L`-loop vacuum family, the complete scalar-product space has

```text
K = L(L+1)/2
```

coordinates and the ordinary vacuum IBPs provide `L^2` source operators
`partial(l_i) dot l_j`. A maximal cubic vacuum graph has `3(L-1)` physical
propagators; the remaining coordinates are irreducible scalar products.

| loops `L` | coordinates `K` | ordinary sources | maximal cubic propagators | ISPs in that parent |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 3 | 6 | 9 | 6 | 0 |
| 4 | 10 | 16 | 9 | 1 |
| 5 | 15 | 25 | 12 | 3 |
| 6 | 21 | 36 | 15 | 6 |

Thus the current “K6 problem” is the three-loop development family. The
eventual six-loop problem is `K = 21`. `K` is neither a topology count nor a
master count.

## Executive architecture

The recommended approach is a **certificate-first, complement-directed
hybrid**:

```text
authenticated multi-parent graph manifest
  -> exact equal-mass families and ordinary-source DAGs
  -> bottom-up sector/guard tasks with immutable lower owners
  -> exact owner complement exposes unbounded standard pairs
  -> Baikov/syzygy/generating-function lanes propose compact source batches
  -> obstruction-guided modular probes select a small target circuit
  -> exact Symbolica lift, denominator clearing, and full source replay
  -> guarded strictly descending RuleCell
  -> deterministic active-cover rebuild and exact complement recomputation
  -> repeat until only an affordable finite typed terminal tail remains
  -> epsilon-depth proof, immutable layer sealing, numerical campaign/artifact
```

The essential inversion is that RustRed does not blindly enlarge a global
seed box. The current exact certificate tells discovery which infinite
directions are still uncovered, and discovery constructs sources specifically
to cut those directions.

## 1. Freeze the complete physical manifest

First declare the finite physical scope: physics-coloured, cubic-expanded
vacuum multigraphs generated by the intended renormalizable theory and their
allowed contractions. It is not the infinite class of arbitrary quadratic
denominator sets. Use Symbolica's graph generation, canonicalization, and
isomorphism machinery to enumerate and quotient this scope. The manifest
representation must explicitly preserve allowed parallel edges, self-loops,
and other pseudograph details rather than silently projecting to a simple
graph. RustRed supplies line/mass colours and verifies momentum routings. It
does not implement a competing graph-isomorphism engine.

This bounds the one-off six-loop production campaign, not the RustRed engine:
the library and its Rust/CLI/Python family interfaces remain generic in loop
count, topology, denominators, and external kinematics.

A single complete-graph-inspired root is not assumed to cover every graphic
or cographic class. The manifest may contain several maximal cubic parents.
Every registered topology must carry an exact contraction and simultaneous
routing witness into one parent coordinate system. The witness includes an
integral unimodular loop transformation with determinant `+/-1`, every
propagator and ISP map, line/mass colours, sector boundaries, measure
normalization, and the resulting unique canonical integral key. The proof
obligation is matcher coverage, not a topology-name switch. Cross-parent
routes are oriented acyclically in the global order. Lower topologies are
reached by pinches and exact factorization/zero owners.

For a six-loop cubic parent, only the 15 propagator coordinates define the
physical pinch lattice, so its raw downward closure is at most `2^15` sectors
before symmetry rather than `2^21`. The six ISP coordinates remain numerator
coordinates unless a separately proved family extension admits positive ISP
powers. RustRed must prove this physical-domain restriction from the family
and ordinary-source boundary structure; it is not an optimization assumption.
A disconnected denominator graph also does not prove factorization when an
ISP numerator contracts momenta across components. A factorization owner must
replay the complete scalar/tensor numerator factorization, or leave the point
for ordinary IBP reduction.

The physical fibre is constructed natively at common mass `m^2 = 1`; it is
not obtained by naively specializing a generic unequal-mass quotient, whose
rank and guard structure can change. `d` remains exact and symbolic. After
reduction, homogeneity restores a common mass by

```text
(m^2)^(sum(master powers) - sum(target powers)).
```

## 2. Replace the global shift lattice by pointed sector monoids

For each index `a_i`, define sector coordinates

```text
x_i = a_i - 1   when a_i > 0,
x_i = -a_i      when a_i <= 0.
```

Then `x` lies in `N^K`. Outward shifts form a pointed monoid with a
well-founded order. Crossing `x_i = 0` becomes an explicit dependency on a
proper subsector. Process the sector DAG bottom-up, installing immutable
scaleless, factorized, symmetry-routed, terminal, and rewrite owners before a
parent sector may use them.

One machine-checkable global order covers the whole manifest, not only a
single same-sector row reduction. Its ordered components include canonical
parent/family orientation, the acyclic sector DAG, same-sector dot/numerator
rank and tie-breakers, symmetry routing, and lower-loop factorization. Products
of disconnected integrals use the multiset extension of the lower-loop order.
Every child is canonicalized first and only then compared. This prevents a
locally descending rule from cycling through a symmetry or cross-parent route.

This sectorization is important algebraically: ordinary monomial divisibility
cannot be applied unchanged to the globally invertible `Z^K` double-shift
lattice. Coefficients also remain Ore-semilinear:

```text
E_i c(n) = c(n + e_i) E_i.
```

A commutative leading ideal is therefore only a coverage index. It never
authenticates a shifted coefficient, an original IBP source identity, or an
exceptional wall.

## 3. The publication certificate

For every family, sector, and exact guard leaf, the artifact must contain:

1. regenerated ordinary-source identities and immutable provenance;
2. exact fraction-free rule identities, including source cofactors;
3. target-pulled applicability guards and the complete guard partition;
4. same-sector strict-descent proofs and explicit proper-subsector children;
5. exact symmetry, zero, factorization, and lower-sector routes;
6. a disjoint axis/guard owner cover of the entire integer domain;
7. an explicit finite complement of typed terminal keys; and
8. a target-rank-independent epsilon-valuation bound.

Use a Janet/Janet-like or Stanley complement to describe uncovered monomial
regions inside each exact semantic guard leaf. A standard pair `(a,F)`
represents `a + N^F`:

- `F != empty` is a potentially unbounded all-rank obligation and cannot be
  called a master unless the accompanying guard-domain proof supplies finite
  bounds in every free direction;
- `F = empty` is one finite candidate terminal, subject to exact ownership and
  numerical affordability.

The monomial complement is never used without the guard domain: a lead cone
with guard `n_1 != n_2` still leaves the infinite diagonal `n_1 = n_2`, and a
nonlinear Pell-type wall may be sparse but infinite. Publication accepts a
finite or empty exceptional branch only with a typed coordinate-bound,
Presburger affine/congruence, zero-dimensional enumeration, modular
impossibility, or algebraic-emptiness certificate with retained cofactors.
Everything else remains `Incomplete`, and the complement is recomputed on
every surviving guard branch.

Prefer exact guard saturation before branching. If several replayed rules
have the same lead and their lead coefficients generate the unit ideal, their
source cofactors can be Bezout-combined into a guard-free lead; for example,
`n_1` and `n_1 - 1` generate one. This can remove an infinite exceptional wall
more cheaply than adding many point rules.

Compile publication ownership into a canonical disjoint axis-block trie plus
a reduced exact guard-decision DAG. Hashes are lookup accelerators only; full
payload equality decides identity. Exact `RuleCell` admission is append-only,
but the active owner cover of an unsealed sector is rebuilt deterministically
from every admitted candidate. Stable semantic priority considers descent
margin, fanout, epsilon debt, guard simplicity, and coefficient size rather
than discovery timing. Only after a sector/guard layer closes are its owners
sealed and made immutable lower-sector feedback.

The closure proof is an induction. Every integral is owned by exactly one
terminal or applicable rule. Every rule child strictly decreases the global
order or enters an already solved proper subsector. Therefore recursive
application terminates in the finite typed tail. This proves spanning without
enumerating a training box.

For a deterministic, possibly nonconfluent rewrite presentation, exact
identities plus disjoint ownership and strict descent are enough. Confluence,
critical-pair exhaustion, commuting action matrices, and an independent basis
are additionally required only if RustRed publishes a canonical normal form
or quotient/action presentation.

## 4. Let the complement choose the next sources

Discovery lanes are tried in this order:

1. ordinary translated IBPs as the correctness baseline;
2. complete Baikov/Gram logarithmic-vector-field generators, critical-point
   syzygies, and compact determinantal/Landau-inspired syzygies to compress the
   dimension-preserving source module;
3. no-raised-propagator-power and syzygy-constrained symbolic operators;
4. generating-function descendants directed along the free coordinates of
   uncovered standard pairs—the leading breakthrough candidate;
5. seedless face, simplex-face, or sparse-tube proposals on stubborn walls;
6. bounded signature/Janet/Ore completion as a fallback, never as an
   unbounded default; and
7. finite quotient/action recovery only after finiteness is proved or when an
   already finite terminal tail requires compression.

Complete Baikov logarithmic vector fields are complete for their stated
syzygy problem, not for lattice closure. Critical-point and compact
Landau-inspired syzygies currently have only lower-loop demonstrations.
Generating-function and recent syzygy-rule methods demonstrate compact
symbolic reductions, but no available result guarantees a practical K21
completion. Every lane is therefore a proposal generator; the certificate
boundary remains unchanged.

Score proposals by predicted cut of uncovered free directions, modular rank
gain, sparse source-cofactor support, guard complexity, coefficient degree and
height, sector locality, symmetry-orbit cost, and exact-lift risk. Record the
score inputs so worker scheduling is deterministic and auditable.

Propose for a block of border targets or one maximal-dimensional uncovered
component, not a stream of isolated lattice points. A useful promotion must
remove a free direction, shrink the complement dimension/signature, simplify
a guard wall, or seal a certified finite residue. Repeated point rules with no
such progress are a scaling failure. A generating-function descendant crosses
the trusted boundary only after it compiles to a finite combination of the
ordinary RustRed sources; a formal infinite series or sampled path agreement
is insufficient.

## 5. Obstruction-guided modular selection

For one target, partition physical columns into target `t`, forbidden harder
columns `H`, and allowed lower columns `L`. A useful source combination must
cancel `H`, retain `t`, and leave only `L`. Over a finite field this exists
when

```text
rank(M_H | M_t) > rank(M_H).
```

On a miss, compute a normalized right obstruction `q` with target component
one and `M_(H,t) q = 0`. A candidate source row `r` can cut the miss only if
`r dot q != 0`. RustRed's inverse-incidence index maps nonzero obstruction
support back to canonical `(ordinary source, translation)` requests.

Use a small block of independent obstructions, not only one vector, and rank
candidates by residual-signature rank. Probes at distinct primes and rational
`d` points are independent. They may share immutable incidence/CSR structure
while their values, reducers, obstructions, budgets, and evidence remain
private. A new epoch must use a strict canonical request superset, a fresh
physical plan, a freshly authenticated maximal stratum, and a fresh sample.

A small obstruction block is a priority device, not a permanent pruning rule.
Rotate it toward a complete obstruction basis over successive fair epochs, or
retain a deterministic breadth-fair fallback, so a candidate orthogonal to the
first block is delayed rather than lost forever.

Stable modular support is discovery evidence only. It never installs an owner
or proves that a missing relation does not exist.

## 6. Exact promotion in Symbolica

After a modular hit:

1. rebuild only the selected sparse circuit over the exact indexed field in
   Symbolica;
2. recover its original source multipliers, directly or by CRT/rational
   reconstruction and interpolation after support stabilization;
3. clear denominators fraction-free and primitive-normalize the identity;
4. replay the source cofactors over every physical column, including columns
   projected away during discovery;
5. remap physical rows and shifts into a compact immutable source view;
6. retain intrinsic family/source conditions and the final target
   coefficient guard;
7. remove an intermediate elimination guard only when the cleared source
   replay proves it cancelled from the final identity;
8. prove fixed-sector and universal sector-monotone descent; and
9. pass the existing guarded `RuleCell` admission before recomputing the
   complement.

Every campaign records source-cofactor support, per-variable and total degree,
reconstruction sample count, coefficient bit size, exact-replay peak RSS,
promotion success rate, and modular-candidate/exact-rule ratio. Stable modular
support is not a degree bound. Sparse interpolation is attempted only after
support stabilizes, and it reconstructs source cofactors as well as the final
rule. A proposal lane whose exact-lift rate collapses is killed even when its
finite-field ranks look attractive.

The durable identity is polynomial:

```text
C_t(n) I(n + alpha_t) + sum_j C_j(n) I(n + alpha_j) = 0.
```

Division by `C_t` happens only where its exact target-pulled guard is proved.
Artifact validation replays this once at the untrusted load boundary; the hot
reducer does not repeat whole-artifact authentication.

## 7. Worked exceptional-wall example

Suppose discovery finds a lead coefficient

```text
c(n) = d (n_17 - 1) + (n_20 - 1)
```

multiplying `E_17`. At target `N = n + e_17`, applicability is not `c(N)` but

```text
c(N - e_17) = d (N_17 - 2) + (N_20 - 1) != 0.
```

With symbolic generic `d`, the polynomial vanishes exactly when both
coefficients vanish: `N_17 = 2` and `N_20 = 1`. If every other coordinate is
fixed, this may be a finite terminal point. If any other coordinate remains
free, it is an unbounded exceptional wall and requires another rule or a
separately proved owner. Sampling generic `d`, checking several lattice
points, or observing successful reductions away from the wall cannot close
that branch.

This pullback and grouped coefficient-ideal semantics applies to every lead.
Cheap affine, univariate, sign-definite, and factored guards get dedicated
proof lanes. A general nonlinear integer wall remains typed `Incomplete`
unless another rule covers it or a sound finite/integer certificate resolves
it.

## 8. Symmetry without false source compression

Graph symmetry proposes task orbits, routes, caches, and terminal
canonicalization. Every route is authenticated by the exact momentum and
denominator transformation. A loop-momentum transformation generally mixes
the `L^2` ordinary derivative/contraction operators, so RustRed must not retain
one arbitrary source per graph orbit. High-rank ISP transport can also expand
polynomials sharply; transformed expression DAGs and caches need explicit
degree, term, and byte limits.

Canonicalization is performed before deterministic owner lookup, and strict
descent is rechecked after the route. Symmetry reduces duplicate work; it does
not create an IBP relation.

## 9. Nonminimal terminals and numerical evaluation

Minimality is optional; finiteness and affordability are not. Track:

```text
t = number of typed terminals,
r = independently certified quotient rank when available,
e = number of evaluator inputs,
m = largest coupled numerical system.
```

Through four loops, Vakint's MATAD/FMFT-backed oracle can evaluate retained
terminal integrals to high precision even when RustRed uses a different
symbolic basis, allowing numerical end-to-end parity. At five and six loops,
AMFlow or another approved offline evaluator would have to provide the one-off
values; this is a future requirement, not an existing RustRed capability.

A redundant terminal set cannot automatically be declared an independent
AMFlow master basis. Either evaluate the actual retained integrals without a
circular RustRed reduction, or recover their exact relation module and
compress them. Maintain a recursion ledger proving that terminal evaluation
does not rely on the same unproved closure claim it is meant to validate.
Because auxiliary-mass flow normally introduces an additional scale, the
evaluator plan must also provide reductions for that enlarged family,
boundary data, and a closed differential system. Record its dependency DAG
and coupled strongly connected components, singularities, epsilon depth,
working precision, conditioning loss, checkpoint/restart behaviour, time, and
RSS.

## 10. Uniform epsilon debt

Strict integral descent guarantees termination for each fixed target but does
not by itself bound Laurent depth as target rank grows. Let `nu(c)` be the
epsilon valuation of an edge coefficient. Seek a potential `p`, uniformly
bounded below by a finite constant `-B` over the entire owned domain,
satisfying

```text
p(u) <= nu(c_uv) + p(v),       p(terminal) = 0,       p(u) >= -B.
```

This gives a uniform lower bound on path valuation. A negative-valuation edge
is not by itself fatal because a mandatory later edge may compensate it. A
repeatable negative-total-valuation cycle/path motif, or an arbitrarily long
negative sequence with no compensating potential, is a hard failure until
rules or terminals are changed. For example, the rank-generic recurrence
`I_n = epsilon^-1 I_(n-1)` accumulates unbounded pole debt as `n` grows.

## 11. Parallel and memory architecture

Use one bounded coordinator and worker pool, not a full family copy per
thread.

Shared immutable state includes the manifest, source/provenance DAGs, sparse
patterns, owner layers, complement tries, symbolic expression DAGs, and
fingerprints. A modular worker owns only field values, reducer state,
obstruction blocks, local candidate scores, and its budget. Group probes while
their request fingerprints agree; split them immediately when campaigns
diverge.

Exact Symbolica promotion uses a separate memory-semaphored queue because its
coefficient swell and native scratch are much larger than modular work. If
process isolation is ever required, memory-map immutable CSR/expression data
and transmit compact task/result identifiers rather than serializing complete
families. Checkpoints retain requests, provenance, pivots, obstructions, and
certificate deltas—not filled matrices.

For `W` workers, measure

```text
M(W) = M_shared + W M_private
```

and keep roughly twofold operational headroom. Authority is merged in stable
task order by the coordinator, so output is byte-identical across supported
worker counts. Workers may discover evidence; they never publish owners.

Closure performance and application performance are separate gates. For
increasing dot/numerator rank, measure unique memoized keys, rule fanout,
expression-DAG nodes/bytes, coefficient degree/height, maximum and total
recursion depth, terminal-coefficient sparsity, wall time, and RSS. A strictly
descending artifact that creates an exponential descendant DAG is not a
practical six-loop result.

## 12. Why rectangular frames cannot be the production strategy

For one-sided total degree `r`, ordinary translated-row counts are
`L^2 binomial(K+r,r)`:

| loops | K | degree 3 rows | degree 4 rows | signed radius 3 rows |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 3 | 6 | 756 | 1,890 | 3,393 |
| 4 | 10 | 4,576 | 16,016 | 24,976 |
| 5 | 15 | 20,400 | 96,900 | 124,775 |
| 6 | 21 | 72,864 | 455,400 | 478,332 |

At K21, signed radius four reaches 5,167,044 rows before symbolic fill.
Those counts are not automatically impossible, but exact rational fill,
provenance, guards, multiple probes, and sector multiplicity make blind global
completion the wrong scaling law. Rectangular frames remain controls and
falsifiers; production discovery must be obligation-directed.

## 13. Promotion gates

### K6: verifier soundness

- close every sector and every Vakint three-loop graph class with zero
  unbounded or guard-incomplete leaves;
- fail closed on diagonal, shared-factor, shifted-guard, nonlinear-wall, and
  disconnected cross-numerator regressions;
- replay all exact sources and negative mutation tests;
- prove one global descent order, deterministic discovery-order/worker-count
  owner covers, bounded epsilon debt, and measured high-rank application
  growth;
- target `t <= 100`, 16 GiB, and two hours for the reference pressure run;
- promote a new proposal lane only if it uses at least two times fewer exact
  rows and 1.5 times less peak RSS than its paired baseline.

### K10: sparse generalization

- first exercise dense planar/nonplanar controls, `K_3,3`, banana, and
  multitheta pressure families, then close the complete relevant four-loop
  multi-parent manifest before claiming architecture promotion;
- require three discovery primes, held-out points, then exact replay;
- record worst-parent and worst-sector complement/guard size, promoted rules,
  source-cofactor bytes, exact-lift rate, artifact size, load/application cost,
  RSS, and wall time;
- prefer fill below 20 times input and kill at 50 times;
- stop bounded multitheta probes above 2 GiB/one hour or 16 exact guard
  strata; and
- reject a normal worker already needing about 32 GiB before K15.

### K15: mandatory design gate

- close a dense 12-propagator/3-ISP parent, banana, and multitheta controls,
  then rehearse the same generic algorithm on the complete relevant five-loop
  parent manifest and every reachable sector orbit;
- forbid a global degree-four rescue;
- kill a lane whose completion degree, guards, cofactors, or fill continue to
  grow without closing free directions;
- require `t <= 1000`, or exact compression when known `t/r > 10`;
- require `m <= 300`, preferably blocks near 100;
- complete a measured 50--100-digit epsilon-depth pilot before a large
  numerical campaign;
- project resources from worst-case, not median, K15 sectors. Degree-three and
  signed-radius-three rows alone grow by about 3.6--3.8 times at K21, while
  maximal-parent pinch sectors grow by up to eight times, giving a raw
  roughly 30-fold sector/frame warning before parent multiplicity, guards, or
  coefficient swell; and
- require substantial hardware headroom rather than merely surviving the K15
  run.

### K21: production only after K15 passes

- freeze the complete multi-parent manifest before any production run;
- begin with bounded structure/proposal dry runs, never a monolithic
  degree-four frame;
- require zero unbounded complement, guard, routing, source-replay, descent,
  and epsilon-potential gaps;
- state whether budgets are global, per family, or per evaluator component;
  target global `t <= 1000`, known `t/r <= 10`, `e <= 100` (at most 300 with
  an exact compression/evaluation map), and per-component `m <= 300`, or
  provide exact maps/splits explaining exceptions;
- complete a measured, checkpointed, noncircular full-depth numerical pilot,
  recording conditioning/precision loss and including the intended
  20,000-digit production depth on at least one representative evaluator
  block; and
- stop on held-out support instability, explosive face/tube growth,
  near-terabyte shared state, a censored mandatory family, non-unimodular or
  cyclic routing, collapsing exact-lift rate, exponential high-rank
  application, unbounded epsilon debt, a circular evaluator dependency, or an
  artifact too large to ship.

## 14. Mandatory falsifiers

Delete one ordinary source, one owner, one lower-sector map, and one guard
branch in separate negative controls. Corrupt an Ore coefficient pullback and
a symmetry route. Test far-away ranks, unlucky primes, equal-mass versus
generic-mass behaviour, repeatable epsilon poles, a compact but enormous
finite box, AMFlow recursion, and every supported worker count. Every mutation
must become a typed replay, complement, guard, descent, valuation, evaluator,
or determinism failure.

Finite-box success, sampled path agreement, stable modular ranks, a plausible
master count, or Vakint numerical parity are valuable validation but never
replace the closure certificate.

## Current implementation boundary

RustRed now has authenticated probe-local growing epochs and a sealed,
crate-private/test-only boundary that can lower one live-plan-bound exact
circuit losslessly into `ParametricRule` plus a compact direct
`SourceViewBatch`. The lowering replays all physical columns, preserves guard
chronology, shares canonical source-owned shifts, rechecks its resource
policy, and has passed an independent authority/soundness audit. It is not yet
wired into owner publication.

It does not yet promote that value into a `RuleCell`, recompute a cover, seal a
solved K6 sector layer, or publish the K6 artifact. Each first K6 sector/prime
probe still has rank 90 on both forbidden and augmented blocks and nominates
roughly 3,586--3,822 broad residual requests, depending on the sector.

The scheduler now keeps that exhaustive residual census separate from bounded
frame growth. It scores only nonzero rows by newly introduced forbidden and
physical columns, obstruction-support overlap, exact row width, and canonical
request identity, then admits at most 32 proposals per fresh epoch. Prospective
column classification uses the exact current guard/owner partition, while an
empty residual census bypasses proposal scoring entirely. Aggregate candidate,
translated-source-term, and prospective-classification work is metered and any
cap is an explicitly inconclusive stop.

On the first K6 dependency sector, the frozen eight-epoch pressure run grows
from 90 to 314 requests, 253 to 413 physical columns, and 918 to 3,055 entries.
Forbidden and augmented ranks remain equal throughout, ending at 279/279; no
hit is found. The exhaustive scans inspect 63,573 candidate rows and 645,012
exact source terms. This removes the catastrophic next-frame inflation of the
old all-residual merge, but it also falsifies scalar one-obstruction ranking as
a sufficient closure strategy: repeated rescans dominate while many admitted
rows are rank-dependent.

The immediate experiment is therefore a compact independent obstruction block
with batch-aware rank gain and evaluated-row reuse, followed by Baikov/syzygy
or generating-function proposals aimed at exact uncovered directions. Exact
hits then exercise the lowering boundary and existing owner-cover machinery.
Only after K6 closure and genuine three-loop Vakint parity, followed by
favorable bounded K10/K15 evidence, may the architecture be promoted toward a
future production K21 campaign. The bounded structural studies remain
research-only evidence before then.
