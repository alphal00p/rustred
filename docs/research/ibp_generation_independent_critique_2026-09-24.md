# Independent adversarial assessment of the IBP redesigns

24 September 2026. This is the second, independent review of the
[whole-system architecture proposal](ibp_generation_architecture_review_2026-09-24.md).
Initial hypotheses were sent before reading that proposal; the objections below
were then put directly to its author. No production algorithm or saved rules
were changed by this review. Separately authorized native diagnostics changed
their query inputs, not the production workload or completion contract.

## Bottom line

**Try one bounded finite-cover falsifier through the existing walker before
building another engine or checking all 406 unbounded activation bands.** The
case for eliminating repeated obligations is strong; the case that a cheap,
checkable invariant exists is still unproved. Rank this experiment first because
it can cheaply reject the most consequential hypothesis, not because its eventual
speedup is established.

I do **not** yet rank fused Apply→Route services above guarded-program reuse.
Fusion may discard useful existing Route deduplication and perform more routing
per parent. Both require measured reuse evidence. New IBP discovery portfolios,
block methods and downstream-aware rule generation are legitimate strategic
redesigns, but they do not explain a saved-rule walk in which discovery is absent.
More workers, more outstanding jobs and more busy cores are not substitutes for
fewer obligations or better completed same-goal wall/CPU/memory costs.

Keep the full 67-owner finite generic entry envelope, uncut descendants, existing
finite terminal policy, concrete-runtime compatibility and portable cold loading.
On-demand repair alone, selected QCD examples, newly declaring difficult inputs
terminal, or accepting a capped prefix would change the requested guarantee.

## Evidence that a redesign must explain

The stopped campaign inspected 8.27 million native regions while retaining
10.41 million pending obligations and emitting 1.447 billion events. Zero
observed frontiers does not settle uninspected work. Its approximately 235GB
peak RSS does not identify the allocation shares of queue, containment index,
coefficient objects, obligation ledger and diagnostic history.

Recent controlled results actively disconfirm simplistic explanations:

- Native fixed-substitution reordering roughly halved matched A11/A12 traversal
  while preserving logical work and reducing CPU use. Eliminating expensive
  operations can matter more than occupancy.
- Moving from 25 to 40 inspectors did not establish a wall-time improvement;
  48 inspectors was slower. The no-crossing duplicate-projection removal was
  correct but yielded no material gain in twelve matched controls. Neither
  available workers nor a hotspot's total profile share predicts an optimization's
  payoff. Counts and timings are scoped in the
  [measured profile record](finite_closure_native_profile_2026-09-23.md).
- A12 performs 62,562 native inspections but emits 1,929,785 successors and
  incurs 250,297,311 containment charges. Of these charges, 53,654,813 are
  conservative reverse-maintenance accounting, not necessarily executed pairwise
  proofs. Route inspections are 38,269, about 61% of native visits, not 61% of CPU.
  Reported coordinator/commit/helper durations overlap and are not an Amdahl
  decomposition. Do not add speculative checks to counters already containing them.
- The all-owner census establishes original-shift L1≤8 and nonpositive
  same-support Δ(A+R) for its potential sign regions. It does not establish
  support filtration, guard coverage, exact rule provenance or routed termination.

The newest diagnostics distinguish abstraction difficulty from missing IBPs:

| One width-six band | Selected / terminal / unresolved cells | Successors | Same / strict-subset support |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Six-active owner | 678 / 1 / 0 | 13,258 | 9,726 / 3,532 |
| Seven-active owner | 1,480 / 2 / 0 | 21,197 | 14,870 / 6,327 |
| Eight-active owner | 1,504 / 1 / 8 | 26,521 | 14,771 / 11,750 |

Independent raw checks verified exact query partitions, original-piece/finish
bookkeeping, source/target shift geometry, unchanged inputs/caps and every
emitted support relation. All same-support edges have Δ(A+R)≤0. Thirty-two
six-active edges have ΔA=+1 and ΔR=−1: **A alone is not the invariant**.
The seven-active result retains two optional-refusal records for 34 counted
attempts, explicitly marked incomplete diagnostic provenance; this is not an
unreported native failure. Pinched target owners are not directly installed in
these four-owner probes; routing was not followed.

All eight unresolved boxes geometrically intersect the documented entry E.
They cannot all be dismissed as irrelevant points outside a future finite cover.
Nevertheless, one fixed entry point from each box selects an existing rule and
finishes one-step application: 353 uniform children, no gaps, problems or
unresolved dispatch. Four points unresolved near rule232 use later rule239.
These are eight sampled successes, not proofs for their parent boxes. They show
that broader symbolic queries can defeat guard classification even when their
sampled targets need no new rule. They do not demonstrate failure of the original
correlated entry query either.

## Five proposals under adversarial tests

### 1. Finite covers: first experiment, not a promised shortcut

The attractive change is from a history of paths to a finite invariant C:
E⊆C, every required selected-rule/route image remains in C or an already
authorized terminal/zero case, and concrete transitions terminate. There is no
requirement to compute the least reachable set if a larger finite set works.
This distinction has a sound precedent in fixed-point abstraction; it is not a
guarantee that an inexpensive abstraction exists. The original
[Cousot–Cousot construction](https://www.di.ens.fr/~cousot/COUSOTpapers/POPL77.shtml)
also separates sound approximation from exact execution semantics.

Three difficulties remain:

1. A short list of regions does not bound the native work inside them. Exact
   P=A+R sublevels admit O(B) native A/R slabs, but their overlaps and broad
   quantified domains can trigger huge guard partitions. A containing rectangle
   A≤B,R≤B is not the same set as A+R≤B. Enlarging C can introduce unreachable
   gaps, missing routed owners and difficult guards; these reject the template,
   not the finite-entry objective.
2. Current inclusion already reuses admitted pending domains. A candidate C
   can therefore be tested through existing query admission and global
   responsibility accounting. A new circular proof-cache engine is not necessary
   merely to recognize an abstract self-edge. Conversely, mutually pending
   regions must not declare each other complete without every local check and
   global obligation being discharged.
3. A finite closed graph can still contain a concrete cycle. Bounded P and
   abstract SCC membership do not prove termination. Check the actual same-owner
   descent, support-changing transitions and route/phase behavior. A valid
   support-layer argument can tolerate P ties; a support-exchanging transition
   must not be silently treated as a strict pinch.

**Disconfirming experiment:** a small, explicitly serialized C containing every
A11 entry, using existing native bounds and unchanged saved programs. Run the
ordinary walker with every escaping descendant retained. Count synthesis,
admission, internal predicates/splits, native work, total CPU, wall, output and
peak retained bytes. This compares the **same required E but different proof
inputs**, not identical native queries. Stop/reject the template when escape,
unknowns or work approach the ordinary control's scale. A failed/capped proposal
is incomplete, not a proof by clipping. The first pilot does not require proving
all 406 unbounded bands beforehand. Even success initially remains the existing
locally-resolved operational result; preserve the separate descent/runtime and
portable-delivery gates.

### 2. Fused transfers and semantic services: account for sharing lost

Canonical lower-owner services could remove repeated intermediate obligations.
But those intermediates currently provide a deduplication point. Fusing each
parent with routing can trade fewer queue records for more repeated native route
evaluations. A service with an ordinary per-owner work queue is also not a new
architecture: that organization, immutable contexts and within-owner concurrency
already exist.

The missing semantic unit is a reusable checked transfer/coverage block across
overlapping parents. Its identity must include family/variable context,
program/order and route epoch, source predicates, exact preimages and retained
conditional dependencies. Preserve original denominators, native zero-term
handling, nonzero-child validity, affine constants, route multiplicity and
conservative images. Do not flatten excluded AND-conjunctions or shrink an image
to make composition convenient.

**Disconfirming experiment:** first establish one-hop semantic equivalence, then
replay a recorded **multi-parent** stream with and without fusion. Report old
Route reuse lost, route evaluations added, destination obligations avoided and
end-to-end containment/algebra costs. A one-parent microbenchmark cannot answer
the sharing question. Reject if it only hides intermediate counters or shifts
work to repeated routing/correlation algebra. Exact finite union subtraction
is not free in the current domain representation; use representable residuals
and a fragmentation-budget fallback rather than an invented general CAS.

### 3. Compiled guarded programs: preserve priority, demonstrate reusable interiors

The artifacts have not lost their cases or guards. Compilation could share
repeated predicate and transfer interpretation, but cannot simply reorder tests
across first-applicable rules or a terminal boundary. The rule232→239 fixed-point
examples make this concrete: an unresolved earlier predicate is not permission
to use a convenient later rule on the entire box.

No complete-query cache should be sold as the solution when the previous A11
native inputs were all distinct. Nor is a recurring fixed-index layout a valid
coefficient/guard proof key without its values, affine context, predicates and
epoch. Source-cell restriction/preimage proofs matter; target-only caches can
cover states never justified by the original proof. An eager globally disjoint
decision partition can reproduce the original explosion at load time.

**Disconfirming experiment:** cold-compile one owner, replay a completed query
stream, and count actual repeated inner operations and bytes saved. Include
compile time, memory and cold reload validation. Require unchanged dispatch,
guard/term failures and conditional images, including overlapping fixed/affine
cases and later-rule fallback. Reject if unique guarded cells dominate or the
compiled representation costs more than replay. A tiny immutable RHS permutation
may be useful infrastructure; four explicit sort samples out of 3,070 are not
evidence of a material standalone gain.

### 4. Downstream-aware rule selection: useful objective, dangerous local score

Few RHS terms or a smaller local shift does not imply cheap closure. A rule can
reduce fan-out but worsen poles, exceptional coverage, routing overcover or the
number of intermediate visits. Swapping a relation also changes priority and
exceptional responsibilities. Old coverage receipts cannot survive that change
merely because the replacement identity is exact.

**Disconfirming experiment:** one genuinely difficult case, two admitted source
orders, equal total discovery budgets; then native exact validation, exceptional
case accounting and the same bounded downstream reduction workload. Measure
discovery plus repeated-use cost, not just first success. Reject improvements
whose extra guard/closure cost exceeds savings. Preserve a Pareto set until
downstream evidence supports selection; do not invent arbitrary new terminals.

### 5. Discovery/reconstruction portfolios: separate lane, not today's diagnosis

RustRed already has shared source definitions, worker-local sector bases, sector
parallelism, modular discovery and exact replay. “Use finite fields” or
“parallelize sectors” alone does not identify a missing capability. The useful
additional hypothesis is bounded independent case/source portfolios and reusable
structural templates, with live coefficient memory and losing work charged.

FiniteFlow demonstrates graph-organized finite-field evaluations and rational
reconstruction, a legitimate source of independent algebra tasks; that is not a
certificate of integer-domain guard coverage. Its reported parallelism cannot
be transferred to the present walk by analogy.
[Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.08019).

**Disconfirming experiment:** profile an actual difficult generation case and
compare exact accepted results per total CPU/RSS under the same allowance. Keep
all exceptional children and exact provenance of the winning candidate; cancel
and account for losing speculative work. Defer this lane for the current stopped
walk unless a real missing target or measured generation cost appears. Cuts or
block systems need an explicit uncut reconstruction/coverage argument, not a
silent omission of boundary sectors.

## Minimum-cost next step and failure boundaries

Prepare a small native C-template for the already completed A11 scope and audit
entry inclusion plus its initial admission before authorizing a run. Use the
existing walker, not a new checker, and retain every escape. Start with a single
bounded falsifier, not an all-owner/all-band campaign. If no representable template
can pass this gate economically, move to measured multi-parent transfer or
guard-program reuse; do not keep widening until the proposed shortcut becomes
the original expensive problem.

The unfinished numerator prototype is optional: canonical numerator extraction
and independent exact specialization at every inactive positive-shift crossing
root are a sound sufficient test, but failures are uncertified, not live
activations. It was preserved unbuilt/unrun when the broader review took
priority; this critique does not certify its draft implementation.

Across proposals, preserve Unknown/invalid-source/exact-gap/cap/cancellation
distinctions; original authority before coalescing; immutable context and version
identity; globally discharged responsibilities; and cold-runtime compatibility.
Keep proof state separate from optional diagnostic history, but measure their
allocation shares before attributing 235GB to JSON. Durable completion must not
precede durable successor obligations. No high-CPU result, one-hop success or
finite list of passing samples supplies the missing full-envelope guarantee.
