# Finite initial queries: four separate parent controls

## Result and scope

All six matched pairs passed: one FG pair, one BMW pair, and forward/reversed
pairs for H and X. Both arms completed their unchanged requested domains,
passed independent all/Off native cold reinspection, and published successfully.
Finite-v3 used its default allowances without a declined case, fallback,
resource stop or censored result. Every pair produced a **byte-identical final
native terminal/normalization state** between ordinary and finite modes.

These are **four separate four-loop parent-own-basis controls**, not the missing
historical common-basis combined `four-all` physics campaign. The existing
native-extracted bundles and exact D8/R≤1/A≤9 query documents were reused. No
IBPs were generated, no bundle re-extraction was performed, and no new helper,
route, ordering or input scope was introduced. H/X each admit one concrete
integral; FG/BMW each admit17. This is a targeted overcover/finite-execution
mechanism test, not broad or unrestricted four-loop coverage.

overlap, but shared-host/memory/filesystem contention was not eliminated.

excluded. `Traversal` is the walk's own reported post-preparation interval.
`Full pipeline` sums supervised walk, independent cold verification and
publication, including wrapper polling/drain overhead. These short phases are
sensitive to the guard's roughly two-second polling granularity. Fresh
processes do **not** mean cold filesystem caches; no cache dropping occurred.

Counts are stable in both H/X repetitions. A finite recipe is **one CP6 record**
but contains thousands of exact operational states; presenting it as a1-vs-87k
work reduction would be misleading. Operational states and symbolic domains are
different units. Here the gains come from changing the traversal/representation,
not demonstrating a large decrease in distinct physical integrals. Normalized
terminals are candidate basis identities, not proven independent masters.

## Remaining qualifications

- This does not fill the historical combined-four-family common-basis gate.
- Every control fits finite admission; it does not measure the v3 scheduler's
  known serialization cost for initial roots that decline and fall back to
  ordinary symbolic work.
- No new numerical master evaluation, terminal-minimization strategy or
  all-input coefficient back-substitution was benchmarked.
- These results support the opt-in finite mechanism and exact publication
  boundary. A full five-loop deployment recommendation must also use the
  separate real9→279-point extension/publication/application evidence and keep
  unsupported finite regions on the ordinary, explicitly monitored path.
